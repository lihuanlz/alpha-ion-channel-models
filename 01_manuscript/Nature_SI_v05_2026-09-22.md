## S1. Model equations and extracted tables

All four models share one construction rule: every voltage dependence is an extracted lookup table (log-linear interpolation: linear in V, logarithmic in the tabled quantity, endpoint clamping outside the measured range). There are no rate equations, no state topologies, no continuous spectra, and no parameter was obtained by global optimisation against the protocols used for validation. Model equations below are numbered (S1)--(S12); the model62 reference formulation equations are numbered (S13)--(S18).

$$\frac{dm}{dt} = \frac{m_{\mathrm{ss}}(V) - m}{\tau_{\mathrm{m}}(V)}, \tag{S1}$$

### S1.3 $Ca_{\mathrm{v}}1.2$ ($I_{\mathrm{CaL}}$). Component-level seal 2026-09-15

**Sealed scope (declared):** the two-input structure of the inactivation block + temperature policy + drug-module architecture. The full-channel forward model (full-voltage $m_{\mathrm{ss}}$/$\tau_{\mathrm{m}}$ tables, recovery branch) is **not assembled**; the gap is registered as a data requirement, not an omission (S6).

**Equations (inactivation block, +17 mV anchor).**
$$I(t) = G\, m(V,t)\, h(t)\, P(V), \tag{S7}$$

with $P(V)$ the permeation factor (ramp chord $G$ is a mixed quantity, see below). For Ba$^{2+}$ (VDI skeleton, no CDI):

$$h_{\mathrm{Ba}}(t) = 1 - f_{\mathrm{VDI}}\bigl(1 - e^{-t/\tau_{\mathrm{slow}}}\bigr), \tag{S8}$$

and for Ca$^{2+}$ (VDI skeleton plus CDI fast component, "add one term"):

$$h_{\mathrm{Ca}}(t) = h_{\mathrm{Ba}}(t) - w_{\mathrm{fast}}\bigl(1 - e^{-t/\tau_{\mathrm{f}}}\bigr). \tag{S9}$$

CDI enters as a constant increment: not graded by current amplitude (C$\alpha$-2 negative), no two-dimensional $h_{\mathrm{ss}}(V, [\mathrm{Ca}^{2+}]_i)$: the "two-input" complexity collapses, at the experimental-design level (Ba$^{2+}$ substitution), into a family of one-dimensional constants.

**S1.4e. Mechanistic identity of the extracted quantities and gold-standard pipeline test (post-seal, 2026-09-26; K$\alpha$-4/5/6; source: Fedida 2024 data pack, Zenodo 10421153).** (i) *Physical identity.* The delay $d(V)$ is the concerted voltage-sensor/pore second transition, not a fitting degree of freedom: VCF F2 fluorescence in the same study gives $\tau_2 = 1/(\delta+\gamma)$ per oocyte, and $d/\tau_2$ has median ratio 0.89 (range 0.79--1.00) across five voltages. The bell-shaped $\tau_{\mathrm{act}}(V)$ is the whole-path rate-limiting current rise, $\approx$ 7$\times$ slower than $\tau_2$; the two time scales have distinct mechanistic referents and are not redundant exponentials. (ii) *Apparent charge and validity window.* Limiting-slope measurements (recomputed per cell from the data pack, matching the published Table 3 to three significant figures) give true gating charge $z_a$ = 2.07--2.47 $e_0$ for $I_{\mathrm{Ks}}$ constructs and 6.08 $\pm$ 0.30 $e_0$ for hERG (n = 4); the single-Boltzmann apparent charge $z = RT/Fk$ (1.1--1.9 $e_0$ for $I_{\mathrm{Ks}}$; $\approx$ 3.4 for hERG) undercounts $z_a$ by a factor 1.3--1.8 in both channels, same direction. This is the structural cost of the single-variable $a_{\mathrm{ss}}$ reduction, not a fit failure. The valid window of $a_{\mathrm{ss}}$ is registered as $P_{\mathrm{o}} \gtrsim 0.01$; below it, allosteric access to multiple open states (the downward bend of the experimental limiting-slope plot) lies outside the $\alpha$ claim, consistent with the pre-existing low-$P_{\mathrm{o}}$ no-claim boundary. (iii) *Gold-standard pipeline test.* The extraction pipeline was run unmodified on the data pack's Scheme V two-tier allosteric model currents (ground truth known). ## S2. Constancy judgement statistics and forward-prediction scoring

**Verdict classes.** Every extracted table receives one of four pre-registered verdicts before any held-out prediction: *constant* (carried as a channel-level table), *per-cell* (carried as a per-cell coordinate), *distributed* (carried as median + measured dispersion), *unusable* (excluded with named artefact). Verdicts are fixed prior to prediction and archived as judgement cards; no verdict was revised after seeing a forward score.

**Constancy test.** For a table entry x measured on n cells, the cell-to-cell coefficient of variation CV is compared against the measurement-noise CV estimated from within-cell replicate dispersion (protocol-specific; e.g., exponential-fit covariance for $\tau$ entries). The entry is declared constant when the two are statistically indistinguishable at the pre-registered level (variance-ratio F test, two-sided, $\alpha$ = 0.05, with the additional operational requirement CV $\le$ 0.30 for family-constant registration). Boundary cases are registered with their CV and n rather than forced (e.g., hERG $h_{\mathrm{ss}}$ levels with n $\le$ 3; Nav $\tau_{\mathrm{h}}(-40)$ with CV 0.40 across the three whole cells, later resolved as a 69-patch distribution, S1.2f).

**Forward-prediction scoring.** Pre-registered accuracy lines per channel:
- hERG sinusoidal forward: per-cell trace-level error within the sealed tolerance in $\ge$ 7/9 cells (observed 7/9; both failures data-side and on record: cells 047 and 060, S6).
- hERG action-potential-clamp forward: $\ge$ 7/9 (observed 8/9; sole failure cell 014 registered model-side: its h tables sit at the population median, individual deviation not captured).
- hERG CiPA third-party reproduction: pimozide-subtraction pool (69 cells assembled, 35--37.5 $^{\circ}$C; one signal-insufficient cell excluded, registered) reproduced in 65/68 (lab3 20/20; lab4 16/16; lab5 29/32); $Q_{10}$ and $E_{\mathrm{rev}}$ sensitivity checks all pass and do not depend on bridge values.

## S3. Identifiability audit of a 13-parameter Markov formulation

### S3.0 Provenance and mathematical form of the model62 reference formulation

**Status.** model62 is our own interim hERG construction (frozen 2026-09-10, "181 endpoint"), documented in the deposited model card (2026-09-11). It is not a delivered product of this work and is not claimed to be a good model. It serves here in exactly two roles: (i) the object of the Fisher-information audit of S3, as a representative of the globally fitted Markov-formulation class; and (ii) an independent-convention reference for two registered comparisons (the fitted reversal potential $-92.9$ mV at 37 $^{\circ}$C, S2; the +40 mV engine anchor chain, S1.1). Its components are literature parts reassembled, and its known defects are documented below and in the archived model card.

**Assembly.** A four-state cyclic scheme (states 1-2-3-4-1, state 3 open; ring topology of the F11 2011 lineage) with opposite-edge equal-rate constraints ($k_{12} = k_{43}$, $k_{21} = k_{34}$, $k_{41} = k_{32}$, $k_{14} = k_{23}$), so that detailed balance holds by construction and only four rates are independent. The activation edge is bi-exponential (the second component follows the Wang 2013 two-charge mathematical form). In parallel with the ring conductance sits a slow component (X) gated at negative potentials. Occupancy dynamics: $dy/dt = M(v)\, y$ with $y_4$ eliminated; protocol voltage applied point by point; the fitting side integrates by Crank-Nicolson at 0.05 ms and the verification side by matrix exponential at 0.1 ms, with a bit-identical cross-check on record.

**Rate equations** ($v$ in mV; rates in ms$^{-1}$):

$$k_{43}(v) = P_1\, e^{P_2 v} + pf_1\, e^{pf_2 v}, \qquad k_{34}(v) = P_3\, e^{-P_4 v}, \tag{S13}$$

$$k_{32}(v) = P_5\, e^{P_6 v}, \qquad k_{23}(v) = P_7\, e^{-P_8 v}. \tag{S14}$$

Slow component: steady state

$$x_{\infty}(v) = \frac{\mathrm{XMAX}}{1 + e^{-(v - \mathrm{VH})/\mathrm{KX}}}, \tag{S15}$$

time constant

$$\tau_x(v) = \mathrm{TAU0}\, \alpha_{\mathrm{tau}}\, e^{Z v}, \tag{S16}$$

negative-potential gate

$$g_x(v) = \frac{1}{1 + e^{(v - \mathrm{GX\_MID})/\mathrm{GX\_K}}} \tag{S17}$$

(73\\% conducting at $-120$ mV, 12\\% at $-90$ mV). Total current:

$$I(v,t) = \left[\mathrm{GKr}\, y_3 + \alpha_{\mathrm{amp}}\, \mathrm{ALPHA}\, x_s\, g_x\right] (v - \mathrm{EREV}), \tag{S18}$$

with $\mathrm{EREV} = -88.33$ mV a Nernst value ($K_i$ 130 / $K_o$ 4 mM, 21.3 $^{\circ}$C), not fitted; the $\alpha$-model of S1.1 inherits this number.

**Parameters.** Sixteen slots, 13 free + 3 frozen ($\mathrm{GX\_MID}$ = -110 mV; $\mathrm{VH}$ = -50 mV; $\mathrm{KX}$ = 13.2 mV), plus four legacy constants inherited from the preceding calibration card ($\mathrm{XMAX}$ = 0.180967; $\mathrm{TAU0}$ = 3.97337; $Z = 7.6390 \times 10^{-3}$ mV$^{-1}$; $\mathrm{ALPHA}$ = $7.4761 \times 10^{-2}$). Free values at the 181 endpoint: $P_1$ = $1.3073 \times 10^{-4}$; $P_2$ = $5.6794 \times 10^{-2}$; $P_3$ = $1.4718 \times 10^{-5}$; $P_4$ = $6.3739 \times 10^{-2}$; $P_5$ = $2.8136 \times 10^{-1}$; $P_6$ = $2.1038 \times 10^{-2}$; $P_7$ = $9.4491 \times 10^{-3}$; $P_8$ = $2.6799 \times 10^{-2}$; $\mathrm{GKr}$ = 0.12868 $\mu$S; $pf_1$ = $9.0621 \times 10^{-5}$; $pf_2$ = $5.9008 \times 10^{-2}$; $\alpha_{\mathrm{amp}}$ = 0.65007; $\alpha_{\mathrm{tau}}$ = 0.44018.

**Calibration and known defects.** Fitted on Beattie cell 16713003 over four protocols (steady activation, deactivation, sine wave, action-potential clamp). Self-set acceptance lines: 7 of 8 passed; the sine-wave $R^2$ of 0.95404 misses its 0.955 line by 0.00096 against a measured data ceiling of $R^2$ $\ge$ 0.973, an architecture-level shortfall rather than noise. Nine-cell cross-validation (all 16 parameters fixed, only $\mathrm{GKr}$ re-scaled per cell) fails: only the calibration cell passes (5 of 6 lines), the other eight cells pass 1-4 of 6, so the parameter set is valid for the calibration cell alone and cannot be carried across cells. A candidate serial-closed-state extension was tested and found unidentifiable (Cramér-Rao bounds $\pm9.6$-13.2 dex against a data-active window of $\pm0.017$ dex) and was not admitted. The full record is deposited (model card 2026-09-11; archive zip containing the 181 minimal run package).

**Object.** A 13-parameter Markov-type hERG formulation from our own interim modelling chain (the model62 reference formulation; provenance, equations and calibration record in S3.0), parameterised by global fit over the four hERG protocol families (activation, inactivation, deactivation staircase, sinusoidal). The audit asks which combinations of the 13 parameters the data constrain.

**Fisher information.** The Fisher information matrix F was computed at the fitted point by automatic differentiation of the Gaussian log-likelihood over all four protocol families, with observation noise estimated from per-sweep residuals. Eigendecomposition gives condition number $\chi = \lambda_{\max}/\lambda_{\min} = 7.4 \times 10^{12}$ with six near-null eigendirections ($\lambda$ below the noise-equivalent threshold).

**Exact degeneracies.** Two null directions are exact algebraic degeneracies, verified symbolically: (i) a joint rescaling of all forward/backward rate constants by factor s together with conductance by 1/s leaves every protocol prediction invariant (rate--conductance joint scaling); (ii) the three parameters $\alpha_{\mathrm{amp}}$, $\mathrm{ALPHA}$, $\mathrm{XMAX}$ enter the model only through their product $\alpha_{\mathrm{amp}} \cdot \mathrm{ALPHA} \cdot \mathrm{XMAX}$, so the two-dimensional fibre holding the product fixed is unconstrained. Double-exponential component degeneracy (interchange of fast/middle branches) accounts for further near-null curvature.

**Modal coordinates.** Projection of F onto its non-null subspace recovers a coordinate system whose entries are precisely the quantities the extraction pipeline measures directly: activation/inactivation/deactivation time constants at the probed voltages, steady-state levels, and delays: the modal coordinates. The mapping from rate constants to modal coordinates is many-to-one along null directions: in a loop-inversion test, 0 of 451 attempts to invert modal coordinates back to the generating rate-constant point succeeded (samples drawn on the null manifold all reproduce the same modal coordinates).

**Practical consequence.** Rate constants quoted from such fits carry at least an order-of-magnitude ($\approx$ 10$\times$) uncertainty along null directions; model cards in this work therefore quote only modal coordinates, with measured cell-to-cell dispersions (main text, Fig. 3a). Order-of-magnitude cross-cell stability of the modal ordering itself: 0.074 dex.

**Slow-memory audit (bounds of any memoryless table model).** At identical voltage, hERG currents differing only in voltage history differ by factors of 17--112 (9/9 cells). The memory spectrum is continuous from 4.8 s to beyond 2,000 s. The present models capture the dominant discrete modes (fast/middle/late components, S1.1) and explicitly do not claim the continuum; this boundary is stated in the model cards and bounds the accuracy of any Hodgkin--Huxley-form reduction.

## S4. Drug-action metrics and component attribution

**Two shape metrics.** For each drug and channel, control and drug segments of the same cell are compared in a ramp window after normalisation:
- **$\varepsilon_{x}$**: cross-protocol discrepancy under a pure-conductance-block model (single rescaling of G). hERG acceptance threshold 0.030 (noise floor 0.004; signal 20$\times$). $Ca_{\mathrm{v}}1.2$ threshold 0.139 = max(null-B p90, 0.03).
- **$\kappa^{*}$**: steady-state shape deviation under a single-scalar rescaling after allowing a temporal warp; $|\kappa^{*} - 1|$ against threshold 0.10, with the fraction of deformation absorbed by warping reported separately.

**hERG (whole-library run; 136/136 datasets judgeable, 517 cell-units).** Pure block rejected: median $\varepsilon_{x}$ = 0.088 $>$ 0.030; concentration drift $\rho$ = 0.387 (p = $3.1 \times 10^{-20}$); 30/30 drugs above the line individually (median range 0.036--0.210; mexiletine strongest, vandetanib cleanest); relaxing the threshold to 0.08 leaves the conclusion unchanged. Deformation attribution: amplitude-shape dominates. Of the judgeable 440 units, median $|\kappa^{*} - 1|$ = 0.020; warp absorbs only $\approx$ 15\% of deformation; residual $\varepsilon_{x1}$ = 0.075 is still above the line. **Drug module pinned: coupling through h ($h_{\mathrm{ss}}$ modulation); $\tau$ tables unmoved.** Registered: $\kappa^{*}$ rises slightly with concentration ($\rho$ = 0.39), a small temporal component below the judgement line; dofetilide deformation almost purely amplitude-type (f_$\tau$ = 0.065); concentration--time confound under incremental dosing registered (resolution needs washout/variant-protocol data).

**Binding kinetics (hERG library base).** dofetilide obeys single-step bimolecular binding ($k_{\mathrm{on}}$ = $1.17 \times 10^{-4}$ /nM/s; $k_{\mathrm{off}}$ = $5.29 \times 10^{-3}$ /s; $R^2$ = 0.781); pimozide does not ($R^2$ = 0.507), indicating multi-step or state-dependent binding; both drugs show trapped-type state preference (negative voltages do not relieve block; $b(-)/b(+)$ medians 0.92--2.91), consistent with inactivated-state preference coupling through the h gate. Perfusion-rate-limit rejected for two slow drugs (E-4031 same-cell reference $R_{\mathrm{E}}$ = 3.4/9.0): measured slow rates are genuine binding kinetics. Library-wide parameter base: 136 datasets, L1 anchoring 124/136 = 91.2\% (9 of 12 failures in lab5, weak-blocker Hill ill-posedness); per-drug $\times$ lab \{$\mathrm{IC}_{50}$, $n_{\mathrm{H}}$, $k_{\mathrm{obs}}(C)$, $R_{\mathrm{E}}$\} panorama sealed; extraction pipeline cross-validated against laboratory anchors ($\mathrm{IC}_{50}$/$n_{\mathrm{H}}$ ratios within [0.7, 1.4], four ratios 0.98--1.02; sweep-wise correlation median r $\approx$ 0.999).
